How to forge a factor

  1. The chalkboard shows a quadratic, like x² + 5x + 6. The tray holds exactly those tiles: x² squares, x bars and 1 units.
  2. Drag a tile onto the bench, or tap a bin and then tap an empty slot. Glowing slots show where your tile fits.
  3. x bars lie flat or stand up. Press Rotate to switch.
  4. Use every tile to build one solid rectangle. Tap a placed tile to send it back.
  5. Press Check. The rectangle's sides are the factors: a side of x, 1, 1 means (x + 2).
